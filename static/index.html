About your results

MedLens helps you organize information from your reports. Range flags use only the reference range printed on that report. Trends appear only when units match; values are never converted.

This tool does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment. Lab results need clinical context. Discuss questions with a qualified health professional.
Your data is stored in your connected MongoDB Atlas workspace. Keep your password private and export a backup before deleting data.
